# Adopted Lessons

Only owner-adopted, evidence-backed guidance belongs here. Keep entries short, scoped, and actionable. Do not copy raw logs, broad model self-evaluations, or temporary task notes.

A lesson should be promoted only after its proposal is `adopted`, its review supports the original hypothesis, and the behavior generalizes beyond one implementation detail.

## Entry format

```md
## LRN-YYYYMMDD-NNN — Short title

- Status: active
- Scope: affected package, workflow, or decision boundary
- Rule: the concise guidance future agents should follow
- Rationale: why the evidence supports it
- Evidence: proposal and commit/test references
- Added: ISO-8601 timestamp
- Supersedes: prior lesson ID or `none`
```
